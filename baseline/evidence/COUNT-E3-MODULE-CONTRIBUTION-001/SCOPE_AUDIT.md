# E3 scope audit

| Evidence family | Coverage | What it identifies | What it does not identify |
|---|---:|---|---|
| K32 Shared full physical ledger | 1000 slices | Paid archive component costs of the full arm | Counterfactual cost after removing a component |
| Support spatial context control | 3 slices | Support-side cost change from no predecessor context to six spatial predecessors | Full production `no_spatial`, because the value branch was held fixed |
| Factorization/sharing factorial | 3 slices | Mechanism-level split and simplified q1-sharing contrasts | Direct production `no_value` or full production `no_sharing` |
| Positive-tail alternatives | 3 slices | Cost of uniform/geometric/negative-binomial tail replacements | Removal of the complete positive-value model |

The correct paper language for the current package is “component-cost ledger plus bounded mechanism evidence.” The stronger sentence “each Shared component causally contributes X bytes” is reserved for the direct five-arm pilot after all arm-specific models and archive members are charged and independently decoded.
